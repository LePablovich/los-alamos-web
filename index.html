WHO WE ARE

We are two beginner developers who started repairing our first game attempt in early 2025. After a huge failure, attempting something too big. In September of the same year, we returned with the intention of making another game, less ambitious, but still looking for something complicated. In December, finally, and as a kind of 'test to ourselves', we started another project, this time very small, which we finally managed to complete in just twelve days. Dunguns became the studio's first official game on December 26, 2025.

Los Alamos Interactive is an indie studio formed by Mr. Danki (developer) and Bris (Direction, art, and sound) with the illusion of making our own video games and, in the future, monetizing with it, although always from the perspective of creating good games, having fun in the process.
