DESCRIPTION

Mix Dungeons and guns and you have "Dunguns", the first game of Los Alamos Interactive made in twelve days. A roguelike game where you have to survive to ten waves. Upgrade your weapon pressing TAB. Infinite waves after Wave 10 if you like. High wave score in the main menu.

Explore a dungeon filled with enemies. Collect coins and upgrade your arsenal to survive waves of increasingly difficult enemies. Can you reach the highest wave and become a legend in the world of Dunguns?
